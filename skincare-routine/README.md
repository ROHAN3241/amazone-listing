## 🚫 Critical Conflict Rules — What NOT to Combine

| ❌ Never Combine | Why |
|-----------------|-----|
| **Vitamin C + Retinol** (same routine) | Different pH requirements destabilize each other. Vitamin C works at pH 2.5–3.5; Retinol at pH 5.5–6. |
| **Vitamin C + AHA/BHA Peel** (same routine) | Over-exfoliation + extreme irritation risk. Both are acids. |
| **Retinol + AHA/BHA Peel** (same night) | Double exfoliation = chemical burn risk. Space 48+ hours apart. |
| **Niacinamide + Vitamin C** (same layer, low pH) | The Minimalist Vitamin C is at a low pH — layering Niacinamide immediately after can form niacin and cause flushing. Separate AM/PM. |
| **Two Vitamin C serums** (same routine) | Don't stack Pilgrim + Minimalist. Use one per routine maximum. |
| **Retinol in the morning** | Retinol degrades in UV light and causes photosensitivity. PM only. |
| **AHA/BHA Peel more than 1×/week** | Over-exfoliation destroys the skin barrier. Saturday only. |

## 📥 How to Import into Google Calendar

### Method 1: Direct Import (Recommended)

1. **Download** the `skincare_routine.ics` file from this repository
2. Open **[Google Calendar](https://calendar.google.com)**
3. Click the **⚙️ Settings** gear icon (top right)
4. Select **Settings**
5. In the left sidebar, click **Import & Export**
6. Under **Import**, click **Select file from your computer**
7. Choose the `skincare_routine.ics` file
8. Select which calendar to import into
9. Click **Import**
10. ✅ All 8 recurring events will appear automatically!

### Method 2: Apple Calendar / Outlook

- **Apple Calendar (iOS/macOS):** AirDrop or email the `.ics` file to yourself, then tap to open — it imports automatically
- **Outlook:** Go to **Calendar** → **Add calendar** → **Upload from file** → select the `.ics`
- **Any CalDAV app:** Import `.ics` directly from file

> **Note:** The .ics file uses floating (no-timezone) times. Events will appear at 7:00 AM and 9:00 PM in whatever timezone your device is set to.

## 📈 Adjustment Timeline

Follow this gradual ramp-up to avoid skin purging and barrier damage:

### Weeks 1–2 (Introduction Phase)
- ✅ Full AM routine daily
- ✅ Niacinamide on Mon/Wed/Fri
- ✅ Hyaluronic + PGA on Sunday
- ⚠️ **Retinol: Tuesday only** (skip Thursday)
- ❌ **Skip the Saturday AHA/BHA Peel entirely**
- Leave Retinol on for only 20 min, then cleanse off (sandwich method if sensitive)

### Weeks 3–4 (Build Phase)
- ✅ Full AM routine daily
- ✅ Add Thursday Retinol
- ✅ Introduce AHA/BHA Peel on Saturday — **5 minutes only**
- ✅ Full Niacinamide/rest day rotation

### Weeks 5–8 (Full Routine)
- ✅ Complete schedule as listed above
- ✅ AHA/BHA Peel can go to **10 minutes maximum**
- Monitor skin — any persistent redness = drop back to Week 1 schedule

### If Irritation Occurs at Any Point
1. Stop all actives immediately
2. Use only: Double Cleanse → Snail Mucin → Hyaluronic + PGA → B12 Moisturizer
3. Maintain this for 1 full week
4. Slowly reintroduce Niacinamide first, then Retinol 1×/week

## 💡 Pro Tips

- **Apply serums on damp skin** (except Retinol — always apply to completely dry skin to reduce irritation)
- **The Snail Mucin** can act as a buffer layer before Retinol if you're sensitive — it slightly cushions the delivery
- **Store Vitamin C + E + Ferulic** in a cool, dark place — it oxidizes rapidly. Discard if it turns dark orange/brown
- **Sunscreen is the #1 anti-aging product** — everything else is secondary. The entire routine is undermined by skipping SPF
- **Retinol tolerance** builds over 4–6 weeks. Mild flaking and tingling in the first month is expected and normal
- **Don't mix Pilgrim + Minimalist Vitamin C** — you have two Vitamin C serums. Use one per routine. The Minimalist 16% CEF is the stronger formulation
- **Saturday Peel:** Always do a patch test on your inner arm the first time. Never use on broken, sunburned, or compromised skin
- **Reapply sunscreen** every 2–3 hours when outdoors, after sweating, or after towel-drying
- **Sleep 7–8 hours** — growth hormone peaks at 11 PM–2 AM and drives skin cell renewal. Your PM routine works harder when you sleep enough
- **Hydrate from within** — drink 2–3L of water daily. Topical hyaluronic acid needs internal hydration to function

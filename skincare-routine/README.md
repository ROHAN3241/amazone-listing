## 🚫 Critical Conflict Rules (What NOT to Combine)

| ❌ Never Combine | Reason |
|-----------------|--------|
| **Vitamin C + Retinol** (same routine) | Different pH requirements — Vitamin C works at pH 2.5–3.5, Retinol at pH 5.5–6. They destabilize each other and increase irritation. |
| **Vitamin C + AHA/BHA Peel** (same session) | Double acid exposure = over-exfoliation + extreme irritation risk. |
| **Retinol + AHA/BHA Peel** (same night) | Double exfoliation = chemical burn risk. Always space 48+ hours apart. |
| **Niacinamide + Vitamin C** (back-to-back at low pH) | Minimalist Vitamin C is at very low pH — applying Niacinamide immediately after can cause skin flushing. Keep them separated (AM/PM). |
| **Two Vitamin C serums** (same routine) | Do not layer Minimalist CEF 16% + Pilgrim together. Pick one per routine. The 16% CEF is clinically superior. |
| **Retinol on damp skin** | Wet skin increases Retinol penetration → higher irritation risk. Always apply to completely dry skin. |
| **AHA/BHA on damp skin** | Damp skin increases acid penetration beyond intended depth → burn risk. Always apply to dry skin. |

## 📈 Adjustment Timeline

### Weeks 1–2 (Acclimatization)
- Use **Retinol only 1× per week** (Tuesday only — skip Thursday Retinol, use Niacinamide instead)
- **Skip the AHA/BHA Peel entirely**
- Let skin build tolerance to Retinol before stacking more actives
- Focus on hydration + barrier repair

### Weeks 3–4 (Gradual Introduction)
- Increase Retinol to **2× per week** (Tue + Thu) if no irritation
- Introduce AHA/BHA Peel on Saturday — **start at 5 minutes only**
- Monitor for redness, flaking, or sensitivity
- If irritation occurs, revert to Niacinamide + Hyaluronic for 1 week and rebuild

### Weeks 5–8 (Full Schedule)
- Follow the full weekly rotation as described
- Peel leave-on time can extend to **10 minutes max**
- Assess skin monthly — adjust if any new sensitivities arise

### If Irritation Occurs
- **Stop all actives** (Retinol, AHA/BHA, Vitamin C) for 1 week
- Use only: Snail Mucin + Hyaluronic Serum + B12 Moisturizer + SPF
- Rebuild the routine from Week 1 protocol

## 💡 Pro Tips

1. **Apply serums on damp skin** (except Retinol & AHA/BHA Peel — always apply those to dry skin)
2. **Snail Mucin as a buffer** — If Retinol is irritating, apply Snail Mucin first and let it dry before Retinol
3. **Store Vitamin C serum** in a cool, dark place — it oxidizes quickly. Discard immediately if it turns dark orange/brown
4. **Sunscreen is the #1 anti-aging product** — Everything else is secondary without it
5. **Reapply sunscreen every 2–3 hours** if you're outdoors — a single morning application is not enough
6. **Set a timer for the AHA/BHA Peel** — 10 minutes is the absolute maximum. Going over risks barrier damage
7. **Pilgrim Vitamin C** is a solid backup serum — Use it when Minimalist runs out, or alternate weeks. Do not use both simultaneously
8. **Skin repair peaks at night** (11 PM–3 AM) — Earlier sleep = better results from your PM routine
9. **Consistency over intensity** — The routine works over weeks and months, not overnight
10. **Photograph your skin** weekly in consistent lighting to track progress objectively
